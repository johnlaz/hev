Baseline Configuration (Do First)

Set these up once so the truck configures itself every time he gets in. Tick each one off as you go.

1. Key Fob & Profile Linking

  • Download the myGMC App, log in, link the vehicle VIN, and pair Phone as a Key.
  • On the center screen, create his User Profile, link it to Key Fob #1, and log into Google built-in (Google Maps / Voice Assistant).

2. Driver Seat, Mirror & Steering Wheel Memory

  • Adjust the seat, power tilt/telescoping steering column, and side mirrors to his driving position.
  • Press SET, then hold Button 1 until a clear beep sounds.
  • In Settings → Vehicle → Seating Position, turn Auto Entry/Exit Seat Recall ON so the seat slides back when exiting.

3. Comfort & Climate Controls

  • Set dual-zone temperature (e.g., 72°F) and press AUTO.
  • In Settings → Climate, turn Auto Heated / Ventilated Seats ON. The truck warms or cools the seats automatically based on cabin temperature.

4. Driver Safety & Updates

  • Set Forward Collision Alert to Medium / Far and verify Emergency Braking is enabled.
  • Check Settings → System → Software Updates to confirm the latest software is installed. (On 2022–2025 trucks that's what brings King Crab Mode; it's built in on 2026 models.)

Shifting & One-Pedal Driving

  • Starting: Foot on brake + push START/STOP.
  • Shifting (PRND): Squeeze the side trigger on the shifter, then push FORWARD for Reverse, pull BACK for Drive, or press P on top for Park.
  • One-Pedal Driving: Turn it on in Settings → Vehicle → One-Pedal Driving. Lift off the accelerator and the truck slows down on its own, so you drive mostly with one foot.
  • Regen on Demand (paddle): Pull the paddle on the back of the steering wheel to slow down without touching the brake pedal. Use it alone or together with One-Pedal Driving.
Good to know: Regen paddle braking isn't recommended on wet, snowy or icy roads, and the brake pedal is still the right tool for hard stops.

Drive Mode Dial Explained

Rotating the center console dial changes suspension height, steering feel, four-wheel-steer behavior, and torque output:

🛣️ Normal Mode Default

Standard ride height, comfortable suspension, light steering. Rear wheels steer slightly opposite the fronts at parking speeds for a tighter turning radius.

🛞 Off-Road Mode

Lifts the truck about 2 inches for dirt roads, sand, and gravel, and relaxes traction control slightly so the tires can spin and keep momentum.

🪨 Terrain Mode Under 15 mph

Auto-Hold Braking: taking your foot off the gas automatically holds the truck so it won't roll back on steep inclines. Smooth creeping through rocks and drop-offs.

🏋️ Tow / Haul Mode

Stiffens the rear air suspension and steering to reduce trailer sway when towing a boat or heavy cargo.

🧑 My Mode

Build your own combo of steering, suspension, acceleration feel and motor sound, then jump back to it any time.

⚡ Watts to Freedom (WTF)

The launch-control mode. Quick, but it's deliberate: the truck makes you confirm several times before it goes.

Closed course only. GMC says to use it only on a closed course in ideal conditions, and the battery has to be above 20% for it to activate.
  1. 1Come to a complete stop and straighten the steering wheel.
  2. 2Press the traction control button on the instrument panel twice.
  3. 3Use the steering wheel controls to confirm the pop-up. The truck lowers itself.
  4. 4Follow the on-screen prompts: press the brake (left foot), then the accelerator, then release the brake to launch.
  5. 5To slow down, let off the accelerator and press the brake. Exit the mode when you're done.

🦀 CrabWalk & King Crab Mode

Note: The truck must be stopped or crawling under 1.2 mph to engage either mode.
  • CrabWalk (diagonal driving): Press & HOLD the 4-Wheel Steer button on the left side of the Driver Mode dial for about 6 seconds, until the CrabWalk animation finishes. The rear wheels then turn the same direction as the fronts so the truck moves diagonally.
  • King Crab (ultra-tight turns): Press & HOLD the right side of the dial (the crab-with-crown icon). The rear wheels turn about twice as much as the fronts. Works up to 25 mph, and it won't engage with Super Cruise or a trailer attached.
  • Exit: Press the left (CrabWalk) or right (King Crab) side of the dial again to go back to automatic four-wheel steer.

🪟 One-Touch "All Windows Down"

To drop all four side windows and the power rear glass together for an open-air cabin:

  • Find the ALL WINDOWS express switch on the driver's door panel (multi-window icon next to the standard window toggles).
  • Push down firmly once: all four door windows and the rear drop-down window lower at once.
  • Rolling back up: pull up and hold the window switches. The rear power window switch is on the main door bank.

☀️ Infinity Roof / Sky Panel Removal & Storage

Taking off the four removable glass panels:

Removal step-by-step

  1. 1Park the vehicle, open the doors/windows, and lower the sun visors.
  2. 2Unlatch the two locking handles on the underside of each roof panel by pulling them outward until they stop.
  3. 3Push UP on the outboard edge of the panel to disengage the vertical alignment pins.
  4. 4Lift the panel up and away from the center I-Bar frame.

Front eTrunk (frunk) storage

  • Open the frunk with the key fob (double tap) or the interior button.
  • Grab the four factory padded storage bags.
  • Place each panel in a bag (weatherstripping facing the rear of the bag) and stack them in the frunk in order. Secure with the integrated floor straps.

📐 Air Suspension & Tailgate

  • Suspension height switch: toggle the console switch to set the height:
    • Entry/Exit: lowers the truck about 2" for an easier step-in.
    • Extract Mode: raises the suspension nearly 6" above normal for clearing water or trail obstacles.
  • MultiPro Tailgate Pickup only: on the Pickup, the top button on the tailgate handle drops the inner gate into a step, work surface, or bed extender. The SUV has a different rear setup.

🟢 Super Cruise (Hands-Free Highway Driving)

  • Drive onto a compatible mapped highway and engage Adaptive Cruise Control.
  • Watch for the steering wheel symbol on the driver's display to turn GREEN.
  • Press the Super Cruise button on the left side of the steering wheel.
  • Steering wheel light bar colors:
    • SOLID GREEN = hands-free driving active.
    • SOLID BLUE = driver override (you're steering/pedaling manually).
    • FLASHING RED = take control of the steering wheel immediately!

🎥 Camera Views & Frunk

  • 360 camera shortcut: tap the Cameras icon on the bottom bar, or press the physical camera switch below the screen, for Surround View and the underbody cameras (with washer spray). There are up to 18 different camera views to explore.
  • Power frunk: double-tap the frunk icon on the key fob, or tap Frunk on the lower-left of the screen to open/close the front trunk automatically.

🔋 Charging & the Energy App

The in-vehicle Energy app tracks charging status in real time and helps pick the best time and place to plug in.

Tip: GMC suggests charging to about 80% for everyday use. It's easier on the battery and leaves room for regenerative braking to put energy back in.
